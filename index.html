CONTEXT

A constitution shaped by democratic transition

  1. 1986The People Power Revolution leads to a transition from authoritarian rule.
  2. 1986–87A Constitutional Commission drafts a new charter.
  3. Feb. 2, 1987Citizens ratify the Constitution through a national plebiscite.
  4. Feb. 11, 1987The Constitution is proclaimed and takes effect.

FOUNDATIONS

Core constitutional principles

Popular sovereignty

Government authority comes from the people, who exercise sovereignty through democratic institutions and participation.

Democratic and republican state

The Philippines is organized as a democracy with representatives and institutions accountable to the public.

Civilian authority

Civilian authority is supreme over the military, reinforcing democratic control of public power.

Checks and balances

Legislative, executive, and judicial powers are distributed to help prevent the concentration of authority.

Human dignity and social justice

The Constitution commits the State to protect human dignity, promote social justice, and work for the common good.

Article I

National Territory

Defines the territory over which the Philippines exercises sovereignty.

Article II

Declaration of Principles and State Policies

States the nation’s foundational principles, including popular sovereignty and civilian supremacy.

Article III

Bill of Rights

Protects civil liberties and sets limits on government power over individuals.

Article IV

Citizenship

Explains who is considered a Filipino citizen and how citizenship may be retained or reacquired.

Article V

Suffrage

Establishes the right and basic qualifications of citizens to vote.

Article VI

Legislative Department

Creates Congress and describes its lawmaking powers and processes.

Article VII

Executive Department

Defines the office, powers, duties, and limits of the President and Vice President.

Article VIII

Judicial Department

Establishes the judiciary, headed by the Supreme Court, and judicial power.

Article IX

Constitutional Commissions

Creates independent commissions for civil service, elections, and auditing.

Article X

Local Government

Supports local autonomy and outlines the organization of local government units.

Article XI

Accountability of Public Officers

Declares public office a public trust and provides mechanisms for accountability.

Article XII

National Economy and Patrimony

Sets policies on national resources, economic development, and Filipino participation.

Article XIII

Social Justice and Human Rights

Promotes social justice and recognizes rights involving labor, land, housing, health, and more.

Article XIV

Education, Science and Technology, Arts, Culture and Sports

Sets State responsibilities for learning, research, culture, and national development.

Article XV

The Family

Recognizes the family as the foundation of the nation and protects its role in society.

Article XVI

General Provisions

Covers matters such as national symbols, language, armed forces, and communication.

Article XVII

Amendments or Revisions

Provides procedures for changing the Constitution through Congress, convention, or initiative.

Article XVIII

Transitory Provisions

Contains rules that guided the shift to the institutions created by the 1987 Constitution.
